## Structural tells

1. **Participial tack-on** (the #1 tell): "The team launched the product, revolutionizing the industry." Comma + -ing tail that adds no info → delete tail or make a second sentence.
2. **Rule of three**: three adjectives, three bullets, three clauses by default. Use two, four, one.
3. **Negative parallelism**: "Not just X, but Y" — state what it IS, skip the imagined correction.
4. **False range**: "From intimate gatherings to global movements" — no real scale exists.
5. **Uniform sentence length**: three consecutive same-length sentences = measurable detection signal.
6. **Parataxis chains**: "Short sentence. Then another. Then another." Use connective tissue.
7. **Hedging seesaw**: pick a side; counterpoint gets max one sentence.
8. **Puffery/significance inflation** (Wikipedia): "marked a pivotal moment", "reflects broader trends", "setting the stage for", "indelible mark".
9. **Emoji formatting** & hashtag stacks (Buffer).
