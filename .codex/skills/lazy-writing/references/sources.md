# Sumber Lazy-Writing

Semua aturan di skill ini dikompilasi dari sumber terbuka berikut (diambil 2026-09-17).

## Riset & rujukan klasik

- **Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing** (WP:AIVOCAB) — 94 tanda tulisan AI; vocab per-era GPT-4/4o/5; puffery; negative parallelism; rule of three; em dash; emoji formatting. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing
- **Russell et al., ACL 2025** (arXiv 2501.15654) — "People who frequently use ChatGPT for writing tasks…" kata delve/tapestry menular ke manusia. https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.15654
- **Kobak et al., Science Advances 2025** — "excess vocabulary" LLM (dikutip repo nusantara & bahasa-skills).
- **George Orwell, "Politics and the English Language" (1946)** — enam aturan: potong kata, kata pendek, aktif, tanpa klise, tanpa jargon. https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/politics-and-the-english-language/
- **Buffer, analisis 52 juta post** — emoji bullet, hashtag stack, struktur template.
